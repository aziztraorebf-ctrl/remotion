THE BEAT — what it must make the viewer feel (~49.5s)
THE CALENDAR TURNS AGAINST BOTH CAMPS. This is where the whole act reverses.
  1. URGENCY NOW — Europe is scrambling to replace Russian gas and secure new suppliers; that is exactly why both camps are rushing to build.
  2. THE TRAP — at the very moment these countries are about to bury tens of billions of dollars in the sand or under the sea, their customer's demand is going to DECLINE. The International Energy Agency is categorical: European demand is bound to fall by 2030.
  3. THE VERDICT — a market that is SHRINKING, and for which a single one of these two pipelines would be amply enough in the long run.
The emotional core is a MISMATCH IN TIME: the money is committed NOW, permanently, into the ground; the demand it was built for evaporates LATER. The viewer must FEEL those two things are out of phase — not merely be told it.
⭐ The hardest craft problem, and the one we will judge you on: THE MONEY IS SPENT AT A MOMENT AND STAYS THERE, WHILE THE DEMAND KEEPS MOVING DOWN. Two different kinds of time on one screen. Solve that elegantly and the beat is won.
