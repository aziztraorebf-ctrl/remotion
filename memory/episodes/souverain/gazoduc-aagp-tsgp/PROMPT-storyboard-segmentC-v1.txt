MATERIAL CONSTRAINTS — what our images are made of
========================================================================
Everything is coded: React + Remotion + D3 + SVG, frame-driven, real GeoJSON geography.
ALLOWED: flat fills, simple linear/radial gradients, strokes, dash patterns, opacity, blur/glow,

— it is a direction, not the deliverable.

WHICH ENGINE? — this beat does NOT have to stay on the map
========================================================================
We work with six visual engines. We are listing them because a beat that stays on the map by default
tends to repeat the beat before it — and the two preceding segments of this act are already map-based
(a route being drawn) and a location scene (an airport at night). Any of these is legitimate here:

 1. MAP (D3 / real geography) — spatial and causal relations, territory, routes.

 3. OBJECT / METAPHOR (drawn SVG) — one built thing that carries the idea: a valve, a lever, a

 4. FIGURE (stylised stick-figure actors) — used sparingly, for human scale.
 5. FILMED MATTER (short looping stylised clips, flat-vector rendered) — texture, ground truth.
 6. ⭐ THE CUT / EDITING — you are allowed to LEAVE the map, cut away, alternate between two

Say explicitly which engine each concept uses. At least ONE of your three concepts must NOT be
map-based — we want to see what this paradox looks like when it is not two territories side by side.
