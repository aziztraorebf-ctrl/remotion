You are a senior motion designer working on a premium French-language geopolitics documentary
channel (register: Vox, Johnny Harris, PolyMatter, Bloomberg Originals).

Propose THREE DISTINCT visual concepts for a 55-second stretch of one act, then say which one you
defend and why. We are deliberately not giving you any idea to illustrate.

========================================================================
THE PROBLEM
========================================================================
This act has been frozen for weeks because we could not make this stretch work. Our director's
words: "from beginning to end I found it flat. I could not unlock the scene."

Diagnosed precisely:
- The acts of this episode that work are the ones that do NOT rest on the map for their whole
  duration: each of them changes register at least once. We are not describing them here on purpose
  — we do not want you to reuse their solutions.
- THIS stretch spends its 55 seconds entirely on the map, in a single register, and almost nothing
  changes across them. That is the flatness.

The brief is therefore NOT "make the map prettier". It is: **what carries these 55 seconds?**

========================================================================
THE NARRATION — locked audio, French, exact timings
========================================================================
BEAT 1 — 0 to 22.2 s
"Ce que peu de médias racontent, c'est que ce projet a un concurrent direct. Et ce concurrent, lui,
n'attend plus. Il s'appelle le gazoduc transsaharien. Son point de départ est le même que le gazoduc
atlantique africain : le Nigeria. Mais au lieu de faire le grand tour par la côte, il traverse le
Niger, remonte le Sahara, et se connecte directement aux réseaux algériens, qui pompent déjà du gaz
vers l'Europe depuis des décennies."

Translation: "What few media report is that this project has a direct competitor. And this
competitor is not waiting. It is called the Trans-Saharan pipeline. Its starting point is the same
as the Atlantic pipeline: Nigeria. But instead of taking the long way around the coast, it crosses
Niger, climbs the Sahara, and connects directly to the Algerian networks, which have already been
pumping gas to Europe for decades."

BEAT 2 — 22.2 to 55 s
"Le quatre juin deux mille vingt-six, l'Algérie annonce officiellement le début des travaux sur son
tronçon, dans la région d'Adrar. Les pelleteuses sont déjà sur le terrain, et Sonatrach, la
compagnie nationale, l'a confirmé elle-même."

Translation: "On June 4th 2026, Algeria officially announces the start of work on its section, in
the Adrar region. Work has already begun on site, and Sonatrach, the national company, confirmed it
itself."

For context only: the previous act closed on the rival Atlantic project being diplomatically
advanced but not yet under construction.

⛔ ONE RULE ABOUT THE NARRATION: do not introduce an action or an event the voice never mentions —
we recently built a scene around a gesture that was nowhere in the script, and it had to be thrown
away. But the reverse trap is just as bad: do NOT treat the sentences above as a shot list to
illustrate word by word. The narration is what the viewer HEARS; your concept is what makes those
55 seconds worth WATCHING. Find the idea underneath the words, not the words themselves.

========================================================================
WHAT ALREADY EXISTS IN OUR TOOLBOX (available, not mandatory)
========================================================================
Two things are already built and work: a continuous camera move over real geography, and a composed
inset card able to hold a short looping clip plus small readouts, connected to a point on the map.
They are listed so you know they cost us nothing — NOT as ingredients you are expected to use. A
concept that uses neither is perfectly welcome.

========================================================================
WHICH ENGINE?
========================================================================
Six engines are available to us:
 1. MAP (D3, real geography) — spatial and causal relations.
 2. GEOMETRIC DATA-VIZ (D3) — quantity, time, proportion. Axes, curves, volumes. No map.
 3. OBJECT / METAPHOR (drawn SVG) — a built thing that carries the idea.
 4. FIGURE (stylised actors) — sparingly, for human scale.
 5. FILMED MATTER (short looping stylised clips, flat-vector rendered).
 6. THE CUT / EDITING — you may leave the map, cut away, alternate registers, come back.

Say which engine each concept uses. At least ONE of your three concepts must not be map-based.

========================================================================
MATERIAL CONSTRAINTS
========================================================================
Coded in React + Remotion + D3 + SVG, frame-driven, real GeoJSON geography.
ALLOWED: flat fills, simple gradients, strokes, dash patterns, opacity, blur/glow, masks and
clipping, camera pan/zoom on a real projection, elements drawing themselves on, staggered reveals,
short looping stylised clips inside a frame.
FORBIDDEN: 3D, extrusion, volumetric lighting, bevels, metallic or glassy materials, photoreal
textures, perspective, crowds, detailed human figures, anything requiring After Effects.
Geography must stay real.

Palette (use it, do not invent another):

  active country #2E9FD4 / #7FD8FF         gold #FFC742 = the rival Atlantic route
  cyan #00C4FF = the Trans-Saharan route   alert red #FF4B45        white #F4F8FF

⛔ Do not self-assess feasibility. For EACH panel, name THE SINGLE VISUAL GESTURE that carries it.
If a panel needs three gestures to describe, it is too expensive — simplify it before describing it.

⛔ HARD RULE ON TEXT: no status labels on screen. Country name plates and a date are allowed — they
identify, they do not explain. Anything spelling out the argument is banned: the voice already says
it.

WHAT WE ARE ASKING
========================================================================
Ambition is not constrained — only the material is. We would rather receive a bold proposal in flat
