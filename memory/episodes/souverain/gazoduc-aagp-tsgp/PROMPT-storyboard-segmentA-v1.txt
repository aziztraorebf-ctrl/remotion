========================================================================
THE PROBLEM WE ARE TRYING TO SOLVE — read this first, it is the whole brief
========================================================================
This act has been FROZEN for weeks because we could not make this stretch work. Our director's
words: "from beginning to end, whether for the financing or for the routes on the map, I found it
flat. I could not unlock the scene."

Here is why, diagnosed precisely:
- Act 2 (which works) is NOT a map act. Over 127 seconds it is: an SVG signature scene, a flashback,
  a financing scene — and only 20 seconds of actual map.
- Act 4 (which works) LEAVES the map entirely for a time-axis and stacked volumes.
- Act 5 (which works) is a valve and a bifurcation. No map.
- THIS stretch is 55 uninterrupted seconds of map: routes drawing themselves across territory, with
  name plates appearing. Nothing else. That is the flatness.

⛔ So the brief is NOT "make the map prettier". It is: **what carries these 55 seconds?**
You may keep the map, but if you keep it you must justify what makes it live for that long.

========================================================================
WHICH ENGINE? — you are NOT required to stay on the map
========================================================================
Six engines are available to us:
 1. MAP (D3, real geography) — spatial and causal relations.
 2. GEOMETRIC DATA-VIZ (D3) — quantity, time, proportion. Axes, curves, volumes. No map.
 3. OBJECT / METAPHOR (drawn SVG) — one built thing carrying the idea: a valve, a pipe in
    cross-section, a ledger, a gauge, a machine. ⭐ This engine just rescued another stretch of this
    same act: a cutaway pipe with two valves and a fluid that pushes but never passes.
 4. FIGURE (stylised stick-figure actors) — sparingly, for human scale.
 5. FILMED MATTER (short looping stylised clips, flat-vector rendered) — texture, ground truth.
    We already have an excavator clip of this kind.
 6. ⭐ THE CUT / EDITING — you may LEAVE the map, cut away, alternate registers, and come back.

State which engine each concept uses. **At least ONE of your three concepts must not be map-based.**
⭐ Strong hint from what has worked on this episode: concepts built on an object that LITERALLY
belongs to the world of gas (pipe, valve, compressor, meter, trench, weld) beat imported metaphors
(bridge, scale, tower) — an imported metaphor forces the viewer to translate before understanding,
and we have measured that cost.

========================================================================
MATERIAL CONSTRAINTS
========================================================================
Everything is coded: React + Remotion + D3 + SVG, frame-driven, real GeoJSON geography.
ALLOWED: flat fills, simple gradients, strokes, dash patterns, opacity, blur/glow, masks and
clipping, camera pan/zoom on a real projection, elements drawing themselves on, staggered reveals,
short looping stylised video clips inside a frame.
FORBIDDEN: 3D, extrusion, volumetric lighting, bevels, metallic or glassy materials, photoreal
textures, perspective interiors, crowds, detailed human figures, anything needing After Effects.
Geography must stay REAL.
