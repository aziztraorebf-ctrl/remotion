## 1. Animation Style Landscape
- Simple styles dominate medieval YouTube: OverSimplified (stick figures), Simple History (minimalist flat)
- Hand-drawn revival trend in 2026 animation (confirmed by prior style research)
- Anti-AI sentiment strong in history community -- audiences value craft and authenticity
- High production =/= high engagement. Personality + storytelling > polish

## 2. Pixel Art + History = Blue Ocean (Unproven at the time)
- Zero creators currently combine pixel art + history education
- Closest precedent: CineFix "8-Bit Cinema" (movie retellings, not history)
- Risk: pixel art audience skews gaming, may not overlap with history audience
- Opportunity: total differentiation if audience crossover works
- Test required before committing

## 3. OverSimplified Vacuum
- Only 3-4 uploads/year, fans actively seek alternatives on Reddit
- "Art of War" = 3.8B views in Shorts format -- history appetite is massive
- Medieval = underserved subtopic (most history channels focus on WW2, Rome, general)
- **Majestic Studios**: 0 to 124K subs in 3 months with AI-generated history content
  - Proves demand exists, but AI approach = risky long-term (anti-AI backlash)

## 4. French Market Gap
- Nota Bene: 2.68M subs (general history, live action + graphics)
- La Folle Histoire: 1.01M subs (humor + history)
- Herodot'com: ~100K subs (medieval specialist, traditional format)
- Animation = almost completely absent in French medieval YouTube
- Gap confirmed at the time: no animated medieval content in French market

## 5. "Medieval > Modern" Contrarian Angle
- MASSIVE viral phenomenon across platforms:
  - @Bitcoin_Teddy: 41K likes on medieval vs modern life post
  - "150 days work per year" medieval fact: fact-checked 3x by Snopes (still viral)
  - r/NoStupidQuestions: 12.4K upvotes on "was medieval life really that bad?"
- Castlecore / medievalcore = active cultural trend (aesthetics + lifestyle)
- Best hook topics: work hours, food quality, health, personal freedom
- Contrarian = engagement engine. Debunking myths = infinite content well

## 6. Medieval Mindset Channel (Benchmark)
- 112K subs, grew fast in ~1 year
- Output: 40 long-form + 116 Shorts
- Angle: contrarian "medieval life was better than you think"
- Format: talking head + historical paintings = very low production cost
- Proves the angle works, but format is easily improvable with animation
