What changed in the entrance

You were right that something was off, and it turned out to be a real technical fault rather than a matter of taste. The meter was actually reaching the floor about two thirds of a second before the impact effects fired. So it landed, sat still, and only then bounced and raised dust — which is exactly why it read as sliding in and then moving up and down separately.

I rebuilt the entrance from the ground up rather than adjusting it:

* It now comes in faster, from the left on a diagonal, and accelerates as it falls the way a thrown object does.
* It lands directly in the approved position — the little readjustment is gone.
* The rebound starts on contact, no delay, and it's one rebound that settles. I also added a brief compression on the chassis at the moment of impact — that's what gives it weight.
* The dust now bursts at the moment of contact, not after.

One judgement call worth flagging: I kept the rebound at exactly the height it had in the version you saw, since that part wasn't what you flagged — only its timing was. Now that it fires on contact, it reads differently: it's part of the landing rather than a separate movement. If you'd like the bounce to carry further, that's a quick adjustment on my end — just say how much.
