COMBINED SEQUENCE BUILD SPEC
Duration: 8.0 seconds. 2D extruded map (slight bevel as in the boards), real coastlines and real province polygons, deterministic frame-by-frame. One continuous camera move. No cuts.

Data lock:
- 1995: 40 volunteers, Luapula only.
- 2005: ~220 volunteers across the seven allowed provinces.
- Allowed active provinces only: Luapula, North-Western, Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern, Lusaka.
- Never highlight, halo, pin, outline, or label: Central, Copperbelt, Muchinga.

────────────────────────────────
PANEL 1 — CONTINENTAL OPEN (from Concept 2, panel 1)
────────────────────────────────
1. TIMECODE: 0.00s → 2.00s
2. CAMERA: Start wide on Africa, map-north up. Continent occupies ~75% of frame height and ~55% of frame width, centered (Madagascar visible, oceans around). Zambia occupies ~8% of frame height and ~5% of frame width, in the southern interior of the continent. Camera is already moving: slow ease-in push toward Zambia’s centroid, plus a small northward pan so Zambia does not sit on the bottom edge as we go in. Speed at 0.00s is low, not zero; by 2.00s the push has clearly begun. No hold.
3. WHAT IS ON SCREEN:
   - Muted grey Africa, textured, no country fills except Zambia.
   - Oceans: dark navy.
   - Zambia: small mid-green country fill (whole country, not provinces). No pins yet (they would be unreadable at this scale).
   - Neighbours unlit grey (DRC, Tanzania, Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, Angola).
   - Lower-left readout: “40 Volunteers”.
4. THE SINGLE GESTURE: The grey continent sits, Zambia already lit green, and the camera starts the push toward it.
5. WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR: Provincial pins, province names, gold country outline, any second country lighting up, a cut, a freeze-frame, globe-spin, Central/Copperbelt/Muchinga callouts.

────────────────────────────────
PANEL 2 — DESCENT ARRIVES + FIRST MARKERS (Concept 1 panel 2 language, loose frame)
────────────────────────────────
1. TIMECODE: 2.00s → 4.00s
2. CAMERA: Continuous push + pan, no cut. By ~3.6–4.0s the working frame is essentially reached: Zambia occupies ~55% of frame width and ~60% of frame height, with neighbour collar still in shot (see FINAL FRAMING). Residual velocity remains (slow creep inward). Do not snap to a stop at arrival. Do not continue into a tight Zambia-fill.
3. WHAT IS ON SCREEN:
   - Africa grey falls away to the edges; DRC (N), Angola (W), Tanzania/Malawi (E), Zimbabwe/Botswana (S) remain as grey context.
   - Water: Lake Tanganyika (NE border), Lake Mweru / Mweru Wantipa (N), Lake Bangweulu (inside north), Zambezi / Lake Kariba (S) as dark blue — real hydrography.
   - Zambia base fill stays green. Central, Copperbelt, Muchinga stay at base fill, no extra light.
   - Markers: Luapula pin+halo becomes readable as soon as scale allows (~2.2–2.6s), already on because that is the 1995 site. Then Northern, then North-Western (stagger ~0.4–0.5s apart). Each marker sits on the interior of its real polygon (centroid is fine; do not invent a lat/lon).
   - Readout still “40 Volunteers” until Luapula is the only lit province; interpolation may begin only as the 2nd marker births (see MARKERS / count).
4. THE SINGLE GESTURE: The camera drops from continent to a loose Zambia-plus-neighbours working view while the Luapula light resolves and the first neighbouring-province lights ignite.
5. WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR: Tight crop that loses neighbours or water; Zambia touching frame edges; pins on Central/Copperbelt/Muchinga; a labelled “150”; camera halt; cut.

────────────────────────────────
PANEL 3 — BODY POPULATION (Concept 1 panel 3 language, same loose frame)
────────────────────────────────
1. TIMECODE: 4.00s → 6.00s
2. CAMERA: Same working view, still creeping (very slow push, maybe 2–4% scale change over the whole panel). No re-center jump. No stop.
3. WHAT IS ON SCREEN:
   - Same loose framing: neighbours and lakes still visible.
   - Earlier pins (Luapula, Northern, North-Western) stay on at full intensity.
   - New pins+halos, in order, ~0.45s apart: Eastern, Western, Southern.
   - Lusaka not yet (or only just starting at 6.00s).
   - The three excluded provinces remain unpinned, unlabelled, un-haloed. If you need them to recede, desaturate/darken those three polygons only, using their real shapes — no fake grey bar.
   - Volunteer readout interpolates 40 → ~220 (not a keyed fake 150).
4. THE SINGLE GESTURE: More yellow pins and halos light across the allowed provinces while the earlier ones stay lit and the count ticks up.
5. WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR: Gold national outline (that was Concept 2 body, not used); name labels on any province; highlighting the excluded three; tightening the frame; a stop or cut.

────────────────────────────────
PANEL 4 — FULL CONSTELLATION (Concept 1 panel 4 language, loose frame)
────────────────────────────────
1. TIMECODE: 6.00s → 8.00s
2. CAMERA: Continue the same slow creep. Velocity asymptotically toward rest only in 7.5–8.00s — never a hard stop at the panel join. End framing = FINAL FRAMING below. No last-second punch-in.
3. WHAT IS ON SCREEN:
   - All seven allowed markers on, each with pin + core + halo.
   - Lusaka (small SE-central polygon) is the last birth, ~6.0–6.5s.
   - Excluded three still unmarked.
   - Neighbours and water still in the collar.
   - Readout settles on “220 Volunteers” by ~7.5s and holds.
4. THE SINGLE GESTURE: The last allowed marker (Lusaka) lands, the seven-light pattern holds, and the count completes at ~220.
5. WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR: Eighth marker; labels on Central/Copperbelt/Muchinga; Zambia filling the frame; fade-to-black before 8.00s; cut; invented extra totals.

════════════════════════════════
THE DESCENT
════════════════════════════════
Continuous camera move. No cut, no dissolve, no jump-cut to a second camera.
Span: begins in Panel 1 (~0.3–0.5s), most of the scale change happens 1.5s → 4.0s, residual creep 4.0s → 8.0s.
Ease-in-out on scale (slow start, faster mid, slow finish). Pan target: Zambia geographic centroid, with a slight bias north so the DRC / Tanganyika stay in the top of frame.
During the descent the map does not change countries: Africa stays muted grey, Zambia stays green. No extra fills. The only additive change is markers becoming resolvable and then igniting, plus the volunteer number. Terrain can gain contrast/detail with scale; do not swap styles.

════════════════════════════════
THE MARKERS
════════════════════════════════
A marker is: a yellow teardrop pin + bright point core + soft radial halo (as drawn), not a naked dot and not a 3D pushpin with a stem into the earth. One per allowed province, parked on the interior of the real polygon.

How many: 7 total. Never more.

Order and timing (visual order, north-first then outward — not claimed as historical sequence; I do not know the real rollout order):
  1. Luapula          readable ~2.3s (implied from 0.00s as the 40-volunteer site)
  2. Northern         ~3.3s
  3. North-Western    ~3.8s
  4. Eastern          ~4.4s
  5. Western          ~4.9s
  6. Southern         ~5.5s
  7. Lusaka           ~6.2s

When later ones appear, earlier ones stay on at the same intensity. No replacement, no dim-out. Optional: 2–3 frame birth pulse, then settle. Halos do not merge into one national glow.

Count: on-screen number is 40 until the second marker. Then interpolate 40 → 220 through ~7.5s. If you display in-between values they are interpolation only — do not treat 150 as data. Do not show other totals.

════════════════════════════════
THE FINAL FRAMING
════════════════════════════════
Operator language: loose medium on Zambia, not a fill. Keep air on every side. No Zambian border closer than ~12% of frame width to any frame edge. Zambia should read as ~55% of frame width and ~60% of frame height, sitting slightly above geometric centre.

Must remain visible in the collar:
- North: DRC, Lake Tanganyika
- East: Malawi (and a hint of Mozambique if the polygon reaches)
- South: Zimbabwe, Lake Kariba / Zambezi
- West: Angola; Namibia/Botswana if the south-west corner allows

Zambia must not go edge-to-edge. Neighbours stay muted grey. That collar is the correction to Concept 1’s tight boards — do not recreate those tight crops.

Unsure (do not invent a fake precise value): exact ease-curve coefficients; exact centroid coordinates (use the polygon library); whether a year string “1995→2005” is wanted (boards show count only — default to count only).
