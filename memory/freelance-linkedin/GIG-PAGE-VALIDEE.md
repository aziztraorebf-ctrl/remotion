## Titre

> I will create a launch-ready explainer video for your startup or product

## Description courte

> Built for founders who need to explain their product fast — before a launch, a pitch, or a landing page.

## Description longue

You know your product. The problem is explaining it in 30 seconds to someone who's never heard of it — for a landing page, a launch post, or an investor update.

I build videos specifically for that moment: how your product works, what problem it solves, why it matters — structured so a stranger gets it immediately. No templates, no stock explainer scenes. Each video is designed from scratch around your product and your message.

If you're not sure how to present your product visually yet, that's part of what I help with.

## How it works

1. You share your product, idea, or script
2. I show you 2 different visual directions to choose from
3. You pick one (or ask me to combine elements from both)
4. I produce the final video

This lets you validate the look and feel early, so revisions later are quick and exact — not a guessing game.

## What you get

- Visuals designed specifically for your product — 2 directions to choose from on every order
- Clear, structured storytelling
- Clean animation, focused, not overloaded
- A process that removes the guesswork before production starts

## FAQ

**Is this fully automated?**
No. I use tools where they make sense, but each video is directed and assembled by me. Nothing here is a one-click template.

**What if I don't have a script?**
Basic/Standard: send me your key points, I'll structure them into a clean flow. Premium: I write the full script with you.

**Can I request changes?**
Yes — revisions are included, and since you approve one of 2 visual directions before I build the final video, most of the "I don't like it" risk is handled early. Revisions cover wording, color, timing — fast and exact. A full change in direction after approval is a new round, not a revision, and I'll always confirm with you before starting extra work.

**Why not just use an AI video generator?**
Because it doesn't understand your product — it gives you something generic and fast. This is built around what you're actually trying to say, and if you need a change next month, it's a quick fix, not starting over.

**I'm not a founder, can I still order this?**
Yes — the process works the same for any product or service that needs a clear explainer. It's just built with founders' timelines and budgets in mind first.
