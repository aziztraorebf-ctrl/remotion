## Titre

> I will create Lottie JSON and SVG animations for your app UI

## Sous-titre

> Animated gauges, meters, loaders and progress states for your app or website. Delivered as Lottie JSON, ready to use.

## Description

Some parts of an interface need to show what is happening. A value filling up. A level rising. A process running. A state that changes. Those are the pieces I animate.

I work from what you already have. Send me a vector file, a screenshot, or a mockup image. You do not need to find the original source files. You get back a clean, lightweight Lottie JSON that your team can add to the product.

**What I do most**
- Gauges, meters and level indicators
- Progress states and loaders
- Live counters and value transitions
- Status changes and small interactions
- Onboarding and splash screens

Every file is built so your team can open it later. Layers are named and the JSON is readable. If you want to change a color or a timing next quarter, you can do it yourself.

## How it works

1. You send your design and tell me what the element should show
2. I confirm the scope and the number of elements before I start
3. I animate it and send you a preview you can play in the browser
4. You get the Lottie JSON, plus MP4 and GIF

## What you get

- **Lottie JSON.** The working file, with named layers.
- **MP4 preview.** To share internally without a Lottie player.
- **GIF.** For docs, email or Slack.
- Transparent background when the element needs it
- Full commercial rights

**About the concepts (Standard and Premium).** I show you 2 or 3 ways the element could move. Not finished animations, just the idea behind each one and what it puts the focus on. You pick one and that becomes the direction. Revisions after that refine it, they do not start it over.

**Please message me before ordering.** Every project is different in scope and budget, and two minutes of chat usually saves a cancellation.

## What I don't do

Better to say it now than after you order:
- **I don't design from scratch.** If the thing does not exist yet, like a mascot, a character, or a concept someone still has to imagine, that is a designer's job. I work from something that already exists, in whatever form you have it.
- **I don't animate logos.** Different kind of project, different approval process.
- **I don't write integration code.** I build and test the JSON. Your developers add the player.

Screenshots and mockups are fine. That is the normal case, and redrawing the element is part of the job. What I do need is a clear view of it. If the object is partly hidden, or if it is a photo of a real object with a lot of moving parts, message me first and we will check it together.

## FAQ

**What do I need to send you?**
A vector file (SVG) if you have one. Figma, Illustrator, Sketch and XD all export to SVG in one click. If you do not have one, a screenshot or a mockup image works fine. I redraw the element cleanly before animating it. Just tell me what it should show.

**Will it work in my app?**
Lottie runs on iOS, Android, web, React, React Native and Flutter with the official players. I test the file before I send it.

**Can I edit the animation later without you?**
Yes, and that is why I deliver this format. Layers are named, the JSON is readable, and your team can change colors, timing or text. You are not locked in.

**How many elements is my project?**
Count the parts that move on their own. A gauge with a needle, a fill and a number is 3. Send me the design and I will tell you before you order.

**What if I need a change after delivery?**
Revisions cover timing, color, easing and spacing. On Standard and Premium you already picked the direction from 2 or 3 concepts, so most of the "this is not what I pictured" risk is dealt with before production starts. Changing direction after that approval is a new round, and I always confirm the cost first.

**Do you do sound?**
The Lottie format cannot carry audio, so the JSON is always silent. If you want sound on the MP4 version, like a click or a soft confirmation, I can add it as an extra. Ask me before ordering.
